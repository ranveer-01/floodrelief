{# ── 01 The problem ───────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}

01 The problem

Relief work is drowning
in duplicates.

When a district floods, requests arrive everywhere at once — helpline calls, forms, group chats, a councillor's inbox. None of those channels talk to each other, so the same household is logged again and again while another is never logged at all.

The cost isn't paperwork. It is a truck sent twice to one address and never to the next street, a donor who can't prove where their money went, and a family that keeps re-explaining an emergency to a new stranger every hour.

The fix isn't more channels.
It's one record that everybody writes to.

03 Who it's for

Four people, one shared record.

  • 01

    Victim

    Files once, gets a reference number, and can check the state of their own request without calling anyone back.

  • 02

    Donor

    Sees verified needs instead of forwarded screenshots, and can follow a contribution through to a delivery timestamp.

  • 03

    NGO staff

    Verifies, assigns and marks delivery from one board — so two teams stop driving to the same rooftop.

  • 04

    Administrator

    Watches the acknowledgement clock across every district and sees where the queue is breaking down.
